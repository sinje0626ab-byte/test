장: 3장: 들너구리의 고뇌
번호: 3

=== 쪽
그림: In the culture office. SCENE: The office of the Minister of Memorial Culture. Deul-neoguri, worn out by the feud between Ttalgi and Hamji, has buried the face in the desk between three large draft memorial portraits of Deul-neoguri for the concert stage. Chumo-tusu bursts in with a paw raised high, eager to speak first - then sees the mood, freezes, and slowly lowers the paw, holding out a carton of banana milk as comfort. COMPOSITION (vertical): Top: the office walls lined with framed gold records, framed concert photos (no text), a shelf of colorful sneakers, a vintage microphone on a stand in the corner. Middle: the big desk, with three large framed draft portraits of Deul-neoguri propped up on it - one smiling, one serious, one gazing into the distance - each with a small black mourning ribbon on the corner; Deul-neoguri's big head is buried face-down on the desk between them, the heavy gold chain clinking on the desk with a tiny sparkle. Bottom right: Chumo-tusu in the doorway, one paw frozen half-raised, the other paw holding out a small banana-milk carton, awkward smile. DEUL-NEOGURI: The big black gorilla in a black suit and tie, heavy gold chain and gold rings; slumped, exhausted. CHUMO-TUSU: The bright-eyed sea otter in a beige suit with a small baseball-glove pin. LIGHT & COLOR: Late-afternoon light, a little gloomy, with warm glints from the gold records. MOOD: Tired and comic - a star at the end of the rope.

문화추모부 장관실.

벽에는 금빛 레코드판들이 줄지어 걸려 있었고, 구석에는 낡은 마이크 스탠드가 서 있었습니다. 책상 위에는 '추모: 더 라이브' 무대에 걸릴 들너구리의 대형 영정 사진 시안이 세 장 놓여 있었습니다. 웃는 얼굴, 진지한 얼굴, 먼 곳을 바라보는 얼굴. 아직 하나를 고르지 못한 채였습니다.

들너구리는 그 시안들 사이에 머리를 묻고 있었습니다. 금목걸이가 책상에 부딪혀 짤랑 소리를 냈습니다. 딸기와 햄지의 대립이 그를 지치게 만들고 있었습니다.

"장관님! 제가 먼저..." 추모투수가 힘차게 손을 들며 들어오다가, 분위기를 보고는 슬그머니 손을 내렸습니다. 그러고는 위로의 뜻으로 바나나 우유 하나를 책상 위에 살며시 올려놓았습니다.

들너구리는 한숨을 쉬었습니다.

=== 쪽
그림: In the culture office. SCENE: Deul-neoguri leans back, sips the banana milk through a straw and quietly explains the real intention: to look back on life while still alive, to turn a life into art - but now it has become a tool of a political fight. COMPOSITION (vertical): Top: a soft, warm daydream cloud (no text) showing a young Deul-neoguri rapping passionately on a tiny stage in a small club, a handful of fans cheering. Middle: Deul-neoguri sitting back in the big chair, sipping banana milk through a straw, gazing up at the memory with sad, gentle eyes. Bottom: Chumo-tusu sitting across the desk, listening with a notepad, eyes sparkling with sympathy. DEUL-NEOGURI: The big black gorilla in a black suit and tie, gold chain and rings; gentle, wistful face. CHUMO-TUSU: The sea otter in a beige suit with a baseball-glove pin. LIGHT & COLOR: The daydream glows in warm sepia and gold; the office below is a cooler, quieter grey-blue. MOOD: Nostalgic and bittersweet.

"딸기 실장은 제 공연을 '전략적 도구'로, 햄지 비서실장은 '신성한 의식'으로만 보고 있습니다. 둘 다 제 예술적 의도는 관심이 없어요."

"장관님의 예술적 의도라면요?"

들너구리가 바나나 우유에 빨대를 꽂으며 천천히 말했습니다.

"저는 그냥... 제가 살아있을 때 제 삶을 돌아보고 싶었을 뿐입니다. 저를 추모한다는 것은 제 삶을 예술로 승화시키는 것이었어요. 하지만 이제는 정치적 싸움의 도구가 되어버렸습니다."

=== 쪽
그림: In the culture office. SCENE: Deul-neoguri does an impression of Ttalgi's flat, expressionless voice, repeating Ttalgi's demand to rewrite the second verse because it scores low with the target age group - then holds up Ttalgi's corrected lyric sheet, so covered in red pen marks that there are more red lines than lyrics. COMPOSITION (vertical): Top: behind Deul-neoguri, a faint ghostly image of Ttalgi (the chubby round white cat in a charcoal-grey suit and red tie) in exactly the same stiff pose, as if being imitated. Middle: Deul-neoguri standing stiffly with a completely blank face, one paw holding a folder flat like a tablet, the other holding up a lyric sheet covered in red slashes and red scribbles (squiggles only, no readable letters). Bottom: Chumo-tusu covering the mouth with both paws, trying not to laugh, cheeks puffed. DEUL-NEOGURI: The big black gorilla in a black suit and tie, gold chain; deadpan imitation face, one eye twitching slightly. LIGHT & COLOR: Cool, flat office light with the bright red of the marks popping out. MOOD: Funny and a little bitter.

"오늘 딸기 실장이 제게 말했어요." 들너구리가 딸기의 무표정한 말투를 그대로 흉내 냈습니다.

"'장관님의 1번 곡은 타겟 연령층 추모 경험치 획득 잠재력이 현저히 낮습니다. 2절 가사를 수정하십시오.'"

그가 서랍에서 종이 한 장을 꺼냈습니다. 딸기가 보낸 2절 가사 수정본이었습니다. 빨간 줄이 어찌나 많은지, 가사보다 빨간 줄이 더 길었습니다.

들너구리가 쓴웃음을 지었습니다. "제 곡을 타겟 연령층으로 분석한다고요. 이게 예술입니까?"

=== 쪽
그림: In the culture office. SCENE: Deul-neoguri points with the chin at a box beside the desk - Hamji's gift, full of gold badges with the President's face, with a note saying to hand them to every audience member. Hamji wants a "President's quotes recitation time" in the middle of the concert, with the audience bowing and chanting. Deul-neoguri wonders whether this is a concert or a religious ceremony. COMPOSITION (vertical): Top half: a dream bubble (no text) of the concert hall where the whole animal audience bows deeply in unison toward a giant portrait of Minju-simin on stage, while a tiny Deul-neoguri stands on the stage holding a microphone, completely confused. Bottom half: the real office - beside the desk, an open box overflowing with shiny gold duck-face badges and a small folded note card (no readable text); Deul-neoguri slumped in the chair, chin jutting toward the box, eyebrows raised in disbelief; Chumo-tusu peeking into the box, holding one badge up. DEUL-NEOGURI: The big black gorilla in a black suit and tie, gold chain; weary, baffled. CHUMO-TUSU: The sea otter in a beige suit with a baseball-glove pin. LIGHT & COLOR: The dream bubble glows with golden, cult-like light; the office is dim. MOOD: Absurd and uneasy - a concert turning into a ritual.

"햄지 비서실장은 더 심합니다."

들너구리가 책상 옆의 상자를 턱으로 가리켰습니다. 각하의 얼굴이 새겨진 금색 배지가 가득 들어 있었습니다. '공연 당일 관객 전원에게'라는 쪽지와 함께였습니다.

"그 사람은 제게 공연 중간에 '각하의 어록 낭독 시간'을 넣자고 했어요. 관객들이 절을 하면서 '진정한 추모는 순수해야 한다'를 외치는 시간을요. 저는 콘서트를 하는 건지 종교 의식을 하는 건지 모르겠어요."

=== 쪽
그림: In the culture office. SCENE: Deul-neoguri picks up the old microphone in the corner and mumbles a sad little rap - "Data on the left, faith on the right, where does my song go, caught in between." Chumo-tusu starts to clap alone, then sees the face and quietly stops. Chumo-tusu asks why not refuse both; Deul-neoguri answers it is impossible - the President ordered cooperation with both - "I just want to survive." COMPOSITION (vertical): A wide, lonely view of the big office in the evening. Top: tall window with the sunset fading to dusk, long shadows. Middle: Deul-neoguri sitting on the edge of a small stage-like platform in the corner, hunched, holding the vintage microphone low, a few small musical notes drooping sadly in the air. Bottom: Chumo-tusu frozen mid-clap, paws apart, awkward and sympathetic; the half-finished banana-milk carton on the floor between them. DEUL-NEOGURI: The big black gorilla in a black suit and tie, gold chain hanging heavy; tired eyes looking down. CHUMO-TUSU: The sea otter in a beige suit with a baseball-glove pin. LIGHT & COLOR: A single soft spotlight of dusk light through the window on Deul-neoguri, the rest of the room in deep blue shadow. MOOD: Melancholy - a star caught between two masters.

들너구리는 구석의 마이크를 집어 들고 힘없이 읊조렸습니다.

"왼쪽엔 데이터, 오른쪽엔 신앙... 그 사이에 낀 내 노래는 어디로 가야 하나... 예..."

추모투수가 혼자 박수를 치려다가, 들너구리의 표정을 보고 조용히 손을 내렸습니다.

"두 사람의 요구를 모두 거절하시는 것은 어떻습니까?" 추모투수가 물었습니다.

"불가능합니다. 각하께서 직접 명령하셨어요. 두 사람 모두와 협력하라고. 저는... 저는 그저 살아남고 싶을 뿐입니다."
